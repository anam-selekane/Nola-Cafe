# PROJECT OVERVIEW
Nola Cafe is a local cafe business that is based in walmer, Port Elizabeth.The cafe was estabilished in 2018, it was created with a passion of bringing people together through great food, desserts and exceptional coffee.
The Nola Cafe website is designed to increase the number of customers and to provide information about the cafe to customers that are outside the province.

# WEBSITE GOALS AND OBJECTIVES
The main goal of Nola Cafe website is to promote the growth and visibility of the business to people around the province and outside.
• Increase website traffic: Attracts more visitors across the province and also outside the province by showcasing the café’s menu offerings and the unique atmosphere.
•	Generate leads: It encourages potential and current customers to connect with Nola Café through contact forms and inquiries.
•	Promote and sell products: Highlight our menu, special and unique food that we offer and how easy they can get the meal through takeaways.
•	Provide valuable Information: Nola Café is based in Walmer Port Elizabeth, the café opens at 7:00am and closes at 19:00 and any menu updates will also be available on the website.
